## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fastest way to afford a Wheelie District new bike?
The quickest route is redeeming all active developer codes while maintaining verified membership in the official *Wheelie District Studios* Roblox group. This grants instant cash injections and an ongoing passive income bonus during stunt sessions.

### Why is my code not working when trying to unlock cash for a new bike?
Codes in *Wheelie District* are strictly case-sensitive and expire without prior notice when major updates roll out. Ensure you copy the exact string, verify that you have joined the developer group, and double-check if the code has migrated to the expired list.

### Does a more expensive Wheelie District new bike guarantee easier wheelies?
Not always. While premium sportbikes boast higher top speeds, supermotos and specialized dirt bikes often feature a broader balance window. For pure vertical stunts and low-speed balance, supermoto models generally outperform heavy superbikes regardless of price.

### Can I sell an old motorcycle after purchasing a Wheelie District new bike?
Game features and resale values depend on current server build versions. Check your garage interface to see if trade-in or resale mechanics are active in your specific lobby before purchasing duplicates.
