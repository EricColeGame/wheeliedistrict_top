## Pro Stunt & Wheelie Control at 200+ MPH

Riding fast in a straight line is thrilling, but executing wheelies at triple-digit speeds is the core identity of the game. Maintaining control on the **Wheelie District fastest bike** requires precise inputs and a deep understanding of weight transfer mechanics.

### High-Speed Wheelie Settings Recommendation

| Setting / Input Parameter | Recommended Value | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brake Sensitivity** | 65% – 75% | Prevents sudden front-end drop during adjustments |
| **Lean Sensitivity** | 50% – 60% | Reduces twitchy handling at speeds above 180 MPH |
| **Throttle Deadzone** | 5% | Delivers instant response when popping the clutch |
| **Wheelie Angle Limit** | Custom / Off | Allows access to scrape angles and 12-o'clock holds |

### Essential Techniques for Speed Runs

* **Clutch-Up vs. Power Wheelie:** At speeds below 80 MPH, use the clutch drop to bring the front wheel up immediately. Above 120 MPH, rely on raw power wheelies by shifting your rider's weight backward while rolling on the throttle.
* **Rear Brake Feathering:** Never hammer the footbrake when balance point testing at 200 MPH. Quick, gentle taps will bring the nose down safely without unsettling the rear suspension.
* **Camera Perspective:** Switch to first-person view or a pulled-back third-person camera to maintain visual reference points along long straightaways.

## Drag Racing Strategies & Gear Ratio Tuning

Winning organized highway pulls and drag races requires tailoring your setup to the distance of the track. Even if you own the **Wheelie District fastest bike**, a poor gear ratio setup will cause you to lose against well-tuned mid-tier bikes.

### Gear Ratio Presets for Different Race Formats

| Race Type | Final Drive Ratio | 1st Gear Setting | 6th Gear Setting | Target Metric |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1/4 Mile Drag** | Short (3.40:1) | Long (Traction focus) | Short (Max RPM at line) | 0–150 MPH Burst |
| **1/2 Mile Roll Race** | Balanced (3.05:1) | Medium | Medium-Long | Mid-range pull |
| **Highway Top Speed Run** | Tall (2.65:1) | Short | Overdrive (Max speed) | 240+ MPH Peak |

### How to Execute the Perfect Drag Launch

1. **Pre-load the RPM:** Hold your staging brake and rev the engine until the turbo spools to peak boost pressure.
2. **Shift Weight Forward:** Keep your rider tucked over the fuel tank to prevent an accidental backflip on launch.
3. **Shift at Redline:** Shift precisely as the shift indicator flashes; short-shifting on liter bikes causes the engine to drop out of its power band.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is currently the absolute fastest bike in Wheelie District?
Based on community testing and maximum upgrade benchmarks, the **H2 Hyperbike** is the **Wheelie District fastest bike**, achieving top speeds of approximately 245 MPH on straight highway stretches when fully modified with Stage 3 parts.

### Can supermoto bikes compete with superbikes in top speed?
No. Supermotos (such as 450cc single-cylinder bikes) are designed for low-speed stunt agility, tight cornering, and slow wheelie combos. Even with max upgrades, supermotos top out around 150–165 MPH, making them significantly slower than 1000cc superbikes.

### How do I stop my bike from wobbling at max speed?
Speed wobbles usually occur due to overly sensitive steering inputs or improper suspension stiffness. To stabilize your ride, lower your steering sensitivity in the settings menu, upgrade to racing slick tires, and ensure your suspension rebound is set to a firmer profile.

### Do cosmetic modifications affect the top speed of my bike?
Cosmetic modifications like custom fairings, exhaust visual designs, and liveries do not impact performance stats. Only parts purchased from the Performance Engine, Transmission, Suspension, and Tire categories alter your top speed and acceleration.
