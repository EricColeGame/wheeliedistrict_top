## Tuning, Upgrades, and Customization Mechanics

Unlocking your vehicle is only step one; dialing in the mechanics is what separates casual drivers from stunt masters. Customizing your **Wheelie District vehicles** allows you to tweak acceleration curves, braking power, and cosmetic elements to match your personality.

```
[Stock Vehicle] ➔ [Performance Tuning] ➔ [Handling Adjustments] ➔ [Aesthetic Customization]
```

### Key Performance Systems to Upgrade

1. **Throttle Calibration:** Adjusting your throttle sensitivity helps maintain the optimal balance point without constantly looping the bike backwards.
2. **Brake Bias:** Rear brake responsiveness is crucial for catching the bike when leaning too far back during prolonged combos.
3. **Weight & Suspension Tuning:** Lowering or stiffening the suspension alters how your vehicle transfers weight under sudden acceleration.

| Upgrade Component | In-Game Benefit | Recommended Setting for Wheelies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Engine Output** | Increases acceleration and top-end speed | High (Sportbikes) / Balanced (Dirtbikes) |
| **Brake Sensitivity** | Prevents over-rotation and crash loops | 75% – 85% responsiveness |
| **Tire Compound** | Dictates road traction and drift slip | Medium-Grip for controlled slides |
| **Sprocket / Gearing** | Alters low-end torque vs top speed | Short gearing for instant wheelies |

Players on handheld devices can also grab the **Mobile Customization Pass (79 Robux)** to adjust their vehicle setups on the fly without needing a full garage pit stop.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best Wheelie District vehicles for beginners?
For new players, dirtbikes and electric minibikes are ideal. They offer lightweight frames, predictable throttle curves, and forgiving balance points, making it easy to learn wheelie physics before investing in expensive sportbikes.

### Can cars do wheelies in Wheelie District?
Yes! While most standard cars provide steady street cruising, specific high-torque muscle builds—such as the gamepass-exclusive Draven Challer Demon 170—are engineered specifically to launch into aggressive wheelies from a dead stop.

### How do I stop looping my bike when pulling up?
Looping typically occurs from applying excessive throttle past the balance point. To correct this, feather your rear brake lightly and lower your throttle sensitivity in the tuning menu to achieve smoother control.

### How do gamepasses affect vehicle progression?
Gamepasses like Double Cash, Double EXP, and Tuning Passes accelerate how quickly you can afford and upgrade new **Wheelie District vehicles**. However, pure stunt skill, timing, and combo chaining remain the primary drivers of success on the streets.
