### Dirtbikes and Supermotos (Motard)
Dirtbikes and motards remain the backbone of the game's stunt culture. Their long-travel suspension soaks up curb drops, while responsive low-end torque lets you pull into a wheelie instantly. Motard supermotos swap out knobby dirt tires for street slicks, providing superior grip and smoother transition angles when carving corners during a one-wheel cruise.

### Sportbikes and Superbikes
Sportbikes are built for high-speed highway pulls and aggressive roll-on wheelies. While they are more prone to looping out if you over-throttle, their top-end velocity generates swift stunt multipliers. Mastering sportbikes requires precise rear brake control to prevent backflipping at 100+ mph.

### E-Bikes, Minibikes, and Scooters
For tight alleyways and technical urban stunts, smaller platforms shine. Minibikes and e-bikes offer tight turning radiuses and virtually instantaneous power delivery. They allow players to practice slow-speed circle wheelies without the constant threat of high-speed wipeouts.

## Top Riding Techniques and Stunt Mechanics

Achieving style points across diverse **Wheelie District bikes** requires mastering three core riding techniques: throttle feathering, brake stabilization, and cornering under lean.

```
       [ Balance Zone ]  <--- Sweet Spot for Multipliers
      /                \
[ Low Angle ]     [ 12 O'Clock ] ➔ (Danger: Over-rotation / Loop-out)
```

1. **The Throttle Feather Technique:** Avoid holding down the gas continuously. Instead, pulse the throttle in rapid bursts once your front tire reaches the desired height. This maintains steady forward momentum without pitching the motorcycle backward.
2. **Rear Brake Modulation:** The rear brake serves as your safety tether. A single tap brings the front wheel down slightly, keeping you safely within the scoring pocket when you lean too far back.
3. **Counter-Steering During Stunts:** Turning while on one wheel requires smooth, subtle inputs. Nudge your steering in the direction of the turn while lightly feathering the throttle to pull the bike through sweeping highway curves.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which of the Wheelie District bikes is best for beginners?
The 250cc dirtbike and standard e-bikes are the best starting choices. They feature balanced power delivery and forgiving balance points, allowing new players to learn throttle and brake timing without constantly looping out.

### How do I stop looping out on high-powered sportbikes?
Looping out happens when you apply too much throttle at the peak of your wheelie angle. To prevent crashing, practice feathering your rear brake key whenever the rear fender gets close to the asphalt, and upgrade your brake components in the garage.

### Do different bikes earn cash at different rates?
Yes. While all vehicles earn cash during stunts, higher-speed machines like supermotos and sportbikes build multipliers faster due to their high velocity. However, slower technical bikes can maintain combos longer in tight city streets.

### Can I customize the appearance of my bikes in Wheelie District?
Yes, players can customize their rides with custom colors, aftermarket parts, wheel finishes, and exhaust upgrades in the garage to create personalized stunt builds.
