## Mastering Wheelie Combos and Stunt Multipliers

The fundamental mechanic for generating cash during open-world roaming is pulling off continuous stunts. Pulling back on your throttle while balancing your rear wheel charges a combo meter that directly scales your payout.

### How the Combo System Works

When you initiate a wheelie, the game evaluates three factors:
1. **Duration:** How long you sustain the stunt without touching the front tire to the pavement.
2. **Speed & Angle:** Leaning near the scrape point at higher speeds generates higher tick values.
3. **Traffic Proximity:** Near-miss bonuses trigger when performing wheelies closely alongside civilian vehicles or environmental obstacles.

```
Base Cash Tick x Speed Factor x Scrape Multiplier x Group Bonus = Total Cash Earned
```

### Practical Tips for Maximum Earnings
- **Avoid Collisions:** A single crash resets your active combo meter to zero, erasing accumulated multipliers.
- **Feather the Throttle:** Tapping instead of holding full throttle keeps your bike locked at the balance point longer.
- **Chain Stunts:** Transition smoothly from a wheelie to a rear-tire drift or endo to keep the combo timer active.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the fastest way to get Wheelie District money as a beginner?
The quickest method for new players is redeeming all active promotional codes for starting capital, purchasing a mid-range Supermoto, and performing straight-line highway wheelies with friends to activate the proximity combo multiplier.

### Does bike speed increase stunt cash rewards?
Yes. Stunts performed at higher speeds award larger cash ticks per second than low-speed tricks, rewarding skilled riders who can maintain balance during fast highway runs.

### Do I lose my Wheelie District money if I crash during a stunt?
You do not lose the currency already stored in your bank account, but crashing immediately resets your current combo streak and forfeits any unbanked multiplier bonus accumulated during that run.
