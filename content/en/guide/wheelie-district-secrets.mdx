### Key Handling Mechanics

*   **Feathering the Rear Brake:** Instead of holding the throttle continuously, pulse your throttle while tapping the rear brake. This keeps your front wheel locked in the 60-degree sweet spot without looping out.
*   **Weight Shifting:** Lean your rider back during low-speed takeoffs to initiate a wheelie at lower RPMs, conserving stability.
*   **Surface Consistency:** Pavement provides predictable friction; avoid sudden curbs or grassy medians while maintaining a high-multiplier scrape.
