### Step-by-Step Stunt Execution

1. **Build Baseline Momentum:** Roll forward in 1st or 2nd gear at roughly 15–25% throttle. Popping a wheelie from a dead stop often results in excessive wheelspin or looping out.
2. **Compress and Pull Back:** Apply a burst of throttle while simultaneously pulling back on your pitch control. 
3. **Locate the Balance Sweet Spot:** Once your front wheel reaches approximately 45 to 60 degrees, feather your throttle to hover in the balance zone.
4. **Modulate with Rear Brake:** If your bike begins to loop backward (over-rotation), tap the rear brake immediately to bring the nose down safely.
5. **Chain Combos:** Shift weight slightly from side to side to initiate swerves and maintain combo multipliers without letting the front tire touch the pavement.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the easiest bike to learn Wheelie District how to wheelie on?
For beginners, the 110cc pitbike or a standard 250cc dirtbike offers the most forgiving balance angle and gentle throttle response. These vehicles allow you to find the center of balance at low speeds without the danger of looping out instantly.

### Why does my front wheel drop immediately after pulling up?
This happens when you let off the throttle completely after initiating the stunt. To keep the wheel up, maintain a steady 40% to 50% throttle input while gently leaning back to stay within the balance sweet spot.

### How do I stop looping out and crashing backward?
Looping out is caused by holding the pitch control too long or applying full throttle while the bike is already at a high angle. The moment you feel the rear fender getting close to the ground, tap your rear brake (`S` on PC or `L2`/`LT` on controller) to lower the nose instantly.

### Do longer wheelies earn more money in Wheelie District?
Yes. The longer you hold a wheelie without dropping your front tire or crashing, the higher your score multiplier climbs. Reaching 60 seconds or more unlocks top-tier cash multipliers, significantly speeding up vehicle purchases and upgrades.
