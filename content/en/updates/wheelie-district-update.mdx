## Mastering the Stunt Economy: How Cash Multipliers Work

Income generation in Wheelie District is directly tied to your technical control. Simply driving forward will net you passive pocket change, but locking into the sweet spot of a wheelie triggers the dynamic combo counter. Understanding the mathematics behind multiplier tiers ensures you maximize your earnings every minute you spend on the road.

```
Base Income per Second × Combo Multiplier × Vehicle Tier Modifier = Total Cash Payout
```

When you elevate the front wheel, your multiplier begins climbing. If you adjust your throttle to keep the rear fender inches from scraping the tarmac without looping out, your combo meter accelerates much faster.

### Multiplier Thresholds and Cash Yields

| Multiplier Tier | Angle Requirement | Time Sustained | Average Cash Yield / Min | Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Basic Lift** | $15^\circ - 30^\circ$ | 1–5 Seconds | \$250 – \$500 | Very Low |
| **Tier 2: Sweet Spot** | $35^\circ - 55^\circ$ | 6–15 Seconds | \$750 – \$1,500 | Low |
| **Tier 3: Fender Scrape**| $60^\circ - 75^\circ$ | 16–30 Seconds | \$2,000 – \$4,500 | High |
| **Tier 4: Apex Combo**  | Variable Angles + Swerves | 30+ Seconds | \$6,000+ | Very High |

### Actionable Tips for Chaining Combos

- **Feather the Throttle:** Tapping your acceleration key rather than holding it down prevents unintentional backflips and loops.
- **Utilize Rear Brake Taps:** If your bike begins tipping past the $75^\circ$ danger zone, quick taps on the handbrake will snap the front wheel back down to a safe angle.
- **Incorporate Gentle Weaving:** Swerving across lanes during a wheelie adds combo points without resetting your current multiplier streak.
- **Find Long Straightaways:** The perimeter highways offer the longest uninterrupted stretches to max out Tier 4 payouts.
