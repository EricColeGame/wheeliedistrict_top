## AI Police Mechanics: How Pursuits Work

The standout feature of the **Wheelie District new update** is the arrival of AI-driven police patrols. Previously, players could hold unlimited wheelies across city freeways without environmental interference. Now, law enforcement vehicles monitor traffic and will actively pursue riders who create public disruptions or pull reckless stunts directly in front of squad cars.

### Understanding the Pursuit System

According to player experience and early community testing, police aggression scales based on your stunt duration and proximity. When you pop a wheelie near an AI cruiser, a pursuit meter triggers, requiring you to utilize alleyways, tight turns, and top-speed runs to shake the tail.

| Police Unit Type | Speed Rating | Agility | Evasion Difficulty | Best Escape Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **City Patrol Cruiser** | Moderate | Medium | Low | Make quick 90-degree alleyway cuts |
| **Highway Interceptor** | High | Low | Medium | Utilize off-road dirt paths or pedestrian walkways |
| **Tactical SUV** | High | High | High | Weave through tight oncoming traffic |

### Tips to Evade AI Cops Without Dropping Combos

1. **Leverage Vehicle Agility:** Cars and heavy cruisers struggle with tight turning radiuses. Use lightweight dirtbikes or motard supermotos to dart between bollards and narrow alley passages.
2. **Watch the Minimap Radar:** Keep an eye out for patrol routes before initiating high-multiplier balance chains.
3. **Feather the Rear Brake:** When turning sharply to evade police roadblocks, lightly tap your rear brake to rotate your bike's rear end without completely cutting your engine momentum.
