## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the best place to practice wheelies on the Wheelie District map?
The Industrial Yard and the Riverfront Promenade are the best practice zones on the **Wheelie District map**. Both areas have wide pathways, minimal AI cop presence, and flat road surfaces that allow beginners to find their balance point without heavy civilian traffic.

### How do I lose AI police cruisers quickly across the city?
To quickly shake off AI police, break line-of-sight by darting into narrow alleyways or cutting through pedestrian barriers in the downtown area. Cruiser vehicles struggle with tight 90-degree turns and cannot fit into the tighter cut-throughs available to two-wheeled bikes.

### Are there hidden areas or secrets on the map?
Player experiences highlight several tucked-away rooftops accessible via industrial ramps, as well as secluded parking garages near the financial district that make excellent meetup spots for stunt crews.

### Does vehicle choice affect how the map feels?
Yes. Lightweight bikes and mopeds handle urban obstacles and curb transitions much better, whereas high-displacement superbikes require long, open stretches like the Highway Ring to reach their full potential without crashing.
