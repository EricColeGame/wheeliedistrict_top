The Canal Basin is widely considered the best practice zone by player experience reports. It features expansive concrete flats with zero traffic and no AI police spawns, making it the perfect environment to dial in throttle feathering and balance points before hitting high-risk city avenues.

### How do I earn money faster on the Wheelie District new map?
To maximize earnings, chain your wheelies along long stretches like the Downtown Boulevard or Highway Ring. Maintain high angles to keep your multiplier climbing, tap the rear brake to navigate turns smoothly, and avoid crashing, which wipes your unbanked run total.

### Which bike class is the most versatile for the new map update?
Motard supermotos offer the best all-around performance. They combine responsive acceleration, excellent balance recovery, and rugged suspension capable of handling curbs, concrete transitions, and tight industrial alleys with ease.

### How do I lose police heat while maintaining my stunt combo on the Wheelie District new map?
